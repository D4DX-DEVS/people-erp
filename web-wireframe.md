01 — INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
Restructured sitemap
The current site has 13 individual project pages loose in one mega-dropdown, “Applications” and “Media” sitting
as orphaned top-level items, and Blogs/Updates/Videos/Gallery split into four separate places for what is really
one content stream. The restructure groups everything by visitor intent: give, get help, get involved, or learn.

KEY IA CHANGES
Consolidate 8 top-level nav items down to 7, cutting the 13-link project mega-menu down to a filterable hub, and adding
a new “Associates” dropdown so funding partners such as NABARD get their own discoverable project list. “Donate”
remains a persistent, visually distinct button (not a plain text link to an external Razorpay page) present on every
template, including mobile.
